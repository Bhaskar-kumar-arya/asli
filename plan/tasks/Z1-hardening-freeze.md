**3. `DELETE /v1/me` implemented** (new `services/account` package + `infra/lib/lanes/z1-account.ts`, auto-discovered by `infra/lib/load-lanes.ts`, no other lane's files touched):
- Queries the caller's cabinet memberships (`cabinets` table GSI1, `USER#<userId>`). For each: if the caller isn't the cabinet's sole OWNER, just removes their Member row. If they are the sole OWNER of a cabinet that still has other (non-owner) members, **refuses with 409** rather than silently orphaning those members' shared cabinet - the caller must transfer ownership or remove the other members first (deliberately stricter than just deleting everything). If they're the sole OWNER and sole member, deletes every item under that cabinet's PK (Cabinet META, Member, Invites, Medicines, Matches).
- Deletes all of the caller's push subscriptions (`push-subscriptions` table, `USER#<userId>`).
- Resolves the caller's Cognito Username from their JWT `sub` via `ListUsers` (the pool signs in by email, so Username isn't guaranteed to equal `sub`), then `AdminDeleteUser`. IAM scoped to the one user pool ARN.
- Reports (`REPORT#<yyyy-mm>`) are untouched by design - L's Handoff already confirmed they carry no `userId`, so there's nothing user-linked to cascade there.
- 12 unit tests (`services/account/src/lib/delete-account.test.ts`) covering: non-sole-owner leaves cleanly, sole-owner-with-other-members refuses (409), sole-owner-and-sole-member deletes the whole cabinet, push subscriptions are removed. `pnpm --filter @asli/account lint/test/build` clean; `cdk synth LaneZ1Stack-dev-z1` (against real `dev-shared` SSM params) clean; **not deployed** (no lane deploys itself past what this session's read-only/approved-mutation budget covered - deploying `LaneZ1Stack-int` is the same `int` redeploy step the earlier Z1 session already did for the other 13 stacks, just needs `cdk deploy LaneZ1Stack-int` added to that list next time `int` is redeployed).
